# ESD & Sustainability

## AI Summary
ESD (Environmentally Sustainable Design) obligations arise from both Clause 55 B5 standards (applied at planning permit stage for all multi-dwelling applications) and NCC H6 (applied at building permit stage). Some councils also have local ESD policies (Cl 22.xx) requiring BESS or STORM tool assessments as part of the planning application. Key issues: stormwater management, solar access, energy efficiency, and waste.

## Common Council / Project Issues
- B5-1 permeability: minimum 20% of site area must be permeable — commonly missed when basement, slab, or large paving areas are proposed; porous paving can count if demonstrated
- STORM / BESS tools: councils such as Melbourne, Moreland (now Merri-bek), Darebin, Yarra require a STORM or BESS report at planning stage; BESS score ≥ 50% is typically required
- Rooftop solar area (B5-3): the roof must include useable north-facing area ≥ minimum size (per Table B5-3) that is not shaded 9am–3pm on 22 June; butterfly roofs and flat roofs with excessive plant equipment can fail this
- Overshadowing of neighbour's solar (B5-2): assessed 10am–2pm on 22 June — different date and time window from B4-3 (POS overshadowing); shadow diagrams need to show BOTH assessments separately
- Solar shading to north windows (B5-4): eave/pergola depth must be sized to block high summer sun while admitting low winter sun — requires a solar geometry calculation
- NatHERS rating: minimum 6 stars per dwelling under NCC H6.1; FirstRate5, AccuRate, or BERS Pro; must be certified by an accredited assessor; submit with building permit
- Rainwater reuse: councils with local ESD policies often require minimum 2,000–5,000L tank for toilet flushing and garden irrigation; plumbed-in connections required
- Waste and recycling (B5-5): councils require bin storage areas large enough for the number of bins (general waste + recycling + food organics in some areas), accessible from street, not adversely affecting streetscape

## What to Check
- Site permeability: calculate (permeable area ÷ site area × 100) — must be ≥ 20%; list all permeable surfaces; confirm porous paving spec if included
- Solar generation area (B5-3): measure north-facing unshaded roof area; check Table B5-3 for minimum area; confirm no shading between 9am–3pm on 22 June from proposed or existing structures
- Neighbour solar shadow (B5-2): are there existing solar collectors on adjoining properties? If so, run shadow analysis 10am–2pm on 22 June; confirm proposed building does not shadow those collectors
- Check council planning scheme: does the local policy (Cl 22.xx) require BESS or STORM? If yes, prepare the tool output for planning application
- NatHERS: confirm assessor engaged; confirm all glazing, insulation, and thermal mass is consistent between architectural drawings and NatHERS model
- Waste storage: confirm bin numbers per council waste management requirements; confirm bin enclosure area and location; confirm accessible path to kerbside
- Rainwater tank: confirm tank size, location, plumbing connection for toilet flushing minimum; confirm overflow discharge to legal point
- NCC H6 Zone 6 (Victoria): ceiling R-value ≥ 4.1 (R5.1 preferred), walls ≥ R2.0 (R2.8 preferred), double glazing standard with low-E coatings

## Draft Response Logic
**B5-1 permeability RFI:** Provide a site plan with permeable/impermeable areas coloured and a table: surface type, area (m²), permeable fraction, contribution. Total permeable ÷ site area. If porous paving is included, provide product data confirming permeability rating.

**BESS / STORM query:** Submit the tool report with the application. If score is below threshold, identify which categories are low and propose improvements (larger tank, higher R-value, solar PV, energy-efficient hot water system).

**Solar shadow query (B5-2):** Provide a shadow study showing the adjacent property's solar collector location, and confirm the proposed shadow lines do not intersect those collectors between 10am and 2pm on 22 June.

**NatHERS — building permit stage:** Confirm assessor details, software used, and star rating per dwelling. Attach the certificate. Note any conditions (e.g. glazing performance spec, insulation type) that must be maintained in the building permit drawings.
