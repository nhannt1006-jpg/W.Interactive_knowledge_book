# Clause 55 — Liveability & Amenity (B3 / B4 Standards)

## AI Summary
The B3 (Liveability) and B4 (External Amenity) standards under Clause 55 (updated by VC276) govern private open space, solar access, daylight, overlooking, overshadowing, and internal amenity. These are the standards most commonly raised in RFIs and VCAT hearings. They apply to two-or-more-dwelling applications and are assessed on a per-dwelling basis. The 2025 Townhouse and Low-Rise Code Guidelines (VC276) restructured and expanded these standards.

## Common Council / Project Issues
- Private open space (B3-5): ground-floor POS must be secluded (not publicly visible) and accessible directly from living/dining/kitchen — councils check this connection rigorously; balcony POS must meet minimum area AND dimension requirements per orientation and bedroom count
- Solar access to POS (B3-6): the setback formula — "boundary of SPOS must be ≥ (2 + 0.9h) metres from any wall to the north" — is frequently misapplied; h = height of the wall, not the eave
- Overlooking (B4-4): the 9m radius / 45° angle / 1.7m floor level trigger is commonly misunderstood; raised decks and mezzanines above 0.8m NGL must also be assessed
- Overshadowing — POS (B4-3): shadow diagrams must be to scale at 9am, 12pm, and 3pm on 22 September; councils check whether the shadow falls on north-facing or ground-level POS; >75% threshold
- Overshadowing — north-facing windows (B4-2): 22 June, 9am–3pm; existing windows only; applies to windows within the habitable room glazing area, not skylights
- Room depth (B3-8): new standard under VC276 — rooms relying solely on one-side daylight must not exceed 2.5× ceiling height in depth; often missed in apartment layouts
- Natural ventilation (B3-10): new under VC276 — cross-ventilation preferred; openable area ≥5% floor area per room

## What to Check
- Private open space: ground = 25m², min 3m wide, direct living area access; confirm "secluded" (not street-visible); check Table B3-5 for balcony minimums by orientation and bedroom count; add 1.5m² if AC unit occupies POS
- Solar access to POS (B3-6): for each dwelling's SPOS northern boundary, calculate (2 + 0.9 × wall height) from any north wall — if existing dwellings to north, their SPOS must not be overshadowed >75%
- Overlooking screens: for any window/balcony > 0.8m NGL — map the 9m radius, confirm habitable rooms and secluded POS of neighbours within that zone; screens must be fixed, ≤25% transparency
- Shadow diagrams: confirm correct date (22 September for POS, 22 June for windows/solar panels); shadow must not cover >75% of adjoining north-facing SPOS at any time 9am–3pm
- Daylight to existing windows (B4-1): new building must not block sky exposure below 1m² at each existing habitable window; requires a sky view angle check
- Internal views (B4-5): in Class 2 buildings where unit windows face each other across communal space <6m — screen or offset required
- Functional layout (B3-7): demonstrate furniture layout for all bedrooms and living areas — min bedroom 3.0×3.0m usable; no door swings into living furniture zone
- Room depth (B3-8): depth ÷ ceiling height ≤ 2.5 for rooms without dual-aspect windows
- Clothes drying: where ground POS provided, a practical drying area must also be provided — no size standard but must be accessible and usable
- Storage (B3-11): ≤2 bedrooms = 6m³; 3+ bedrooms = 8m³; internal or external; not under-stair space; accessible

## Draft Response Logic
**Overlooking RFI:** Provide an annotated plan showing: (1) all windows/balconies >0.8m NGL, (2) the 9m radius drawn from each, (3) the neighbour's habitable rooms and SPOS within that zone, (4) the screening or offsetting solution proposed. Confirm screen specs: fixed, ≤25% transparency, extend to full height of opening.

**Shadow diagram query:** Confirm diagrams show existing shadows (dashed) and proposed shadows (solid) at 9am, 12pm, 3pm on the correct dates. Confirm the adjacent SPOS is correctly located and its north-facing area is identified. State the % overshadowed at worst case time.

**POS query — balcony:** Reference Table B3-5 and state the dwelling's bedroom count, balcony orientation, and resulting minimum area/dimension requirement. Show that the connecting door from living/dining meets the 'direct access' test.

**B3-6 solar access calculation:** State the wall height (h), the required setback formula result (2 + 0.9h), the actual setback provided, and whether it complies. Note if the relevant wall is an existing neighbour's wall vs new construction.
