# Building Regulations — Structure (Sub & Superstructure)

## AI Summary
Structural elements (footings, slabs, framing, roof, fire separation) are governed primarily by the NCC and Australian Standards at building permit stage. These are WD (Working Drawings / Building Permit) items, not planning permit items, but they influence site layout, basement feasibility, and party wall design from early stages. Structural engineering is typically required for reactive soil sites, multi-storey construction, and Class 2 buildings.

## Common Council / Project Issues
- Soil classification: reactive soils (M, H1, H2, E) are common in Victoria's eastern and northern suburbs; Class A sites are rare; always obtain a soil test before issuing tender or fixing slab type
- Raft vs strip footing: raft slabs are now standard on most Melbourne residential sites due to reactive soils; prescriptive NCC design only works for Class A/S soils
- Bored piers: required on steep sites, problem soils (Class P), and where fill depths are variable; connection details at top of pier are critical
- Retaining walls >1m: building permit required if within 1.5m of boundary or where height >1m; structural engineer design required; common omission on sloped sites
- Fire-rated walls (FRL): in Class 2 buildings, FRL requirements between sole-occupancy units (60/60/60 or 90/90/90) must be resolved at framing stage; changes after framing are costly
- Fire door certification: AS 1905.1 test certificates must be on file; self-closing mechanism must be maintained; a common defect at occupancy inspection
- Penetrations through fire walls: every penetration (pipe, conduit, duct) must have a tested fire-stop system; councils and building surveyors check this at inspection

## What to Check
- Obtain a soil test report and confirm AS 2870 soil classification before commencing footing design
- Confirm whether NCC prescriptive footing design (NCC H1) is applicable or whether engineer design is required (H1, H2, E, or P soils require engineer)
- For retaining walls: confirm height, proximity to boundary, and proposed system; check Building Regulations r.71 for permit triggers
- For Class 1 timber framing: confirm wind class (TC2, TC3, or TC4+ for coastal/exposed sites) and span tables per AS 1684; provide framing plans with building permit
- For Class 2 party walls: confirm required FRL from NCC C2.3 table; confirm that the proposed wall system (masonry, CSR Gyprock, etc.) has a tested FRL certificate
- Fire doors: confirm the schedule of fire doors, FRL rating, and supplier test certificates; confirm self-closing devices are specified
- Penetrations: confirm that fire-stop systems for all services penetrations are specified in the services drawings; list tested system brands for the building surveyor

## Draft Response Logic
**Soil classification query:** Confirm the soil classification from the site geotechnical report. If M or higher, confirm that the footing system has been designed by a structural engineer per AS 2870. Provide the engineer's details and confirmation that the design accounts for the site's reactive classification.

**Footing system query:** State the proposed footing type (strip/raft/bored pier), confirm the soil classification it responds to, and reference the engineer's report or NCC prescriptive compliance path (Class A/S only).

**Fire rating query:** Provide the NCC C2.3 FRL schedule, confirm the proposed wall/floor/ceiling system, and attach the system's tested FRL certificate (manufacturer's fire test report). Confirm the building surveyor has sighted and accepted the system.

**Penetration query:** List all penetrations through fire-rated elements by location, service type, and proposed fire-stop system. Confirm each system is tested (AS/NZS 4072.1) and that installation will be by an experienced contractor.
