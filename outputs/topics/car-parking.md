# Car Parking, Access & Bicycle

## AI Summary
Car parking is governed by Clause 52.06 (number of spaces) and Clause 55 B2-6 / B3-2 (physical design and streetscape integration). Bicycle parking is addressed by Clause 52.34. Both are common council and conditions in planning permits. Access geometry (driveway grades, swept paths, sight lines) is critical at both planning and building permit stages.

## Common Council / Project Issues
- Parking shortfall: councils commonly object to reduced visitor parking or shared spaces; Clause 52.06-5 allows reduction if criteria met but requires justification (public transport accessibility, nearby parking, sustainable transport)
- Dominant garage frontage (B3-2): a double-width garage on a narrow lot with no other articulation is a frequent refusal ground; councils want to see active frontage — windows, entry, landscaping alongside garage
- Vehicle crossover width and number: most councils allow only one crossover per lot; width typically 3–4m; loss of on-street parking spaces requires justification
- Driveway grades: max 1:5 (20%) on main grade; transition from street to driveway: 1:8 (12.5%); from driveway to garage: 1:5; non-compliance with AS 2890.1 is a building permit rejection risk
- Swept paths: for tandem or multi-storey carparks, AS 2890.6 swept paths must be demonstrated for both B99 car and ambulance; councils request AutoTURN or similar output
- Accessible parking: required for Class 2 buildings; must be on an accessible path to building entry; shared zone 2.4m wide alongside the space
- Bicycle: Cl 52.34 — residential dwellings require 1 secure covered bicycle space per dwelling; Class 2 may also need visitor racks; check council local policy

## What to Check
- Parking numbers: Cl 52.06 rates — 1 space per 1-bedroom, 2 spaces per 2+ bedroom; visitor parking rate per council policy (often 1 per 5 dwellings); confirm if any reduction or waiver is applied
- Parking reduction justification (Cl 52.06-5): proximity to train/tram/bus stops (<400m), on-street parking availability, TDM measures
- Vehicle crossover: confirm single crossover per lot; width 3–4m; submit consent application to council (separate from planning permit in some municipalities)
- Driveway geometry: draw a cross-section showing grade changes; confirm max 1:5 overall, 1:8 transition at each end; headroom in garage ≥ 2.2m min
- B3-2 parking location: confirm parking is behind building line or, if at frontage, is visually balanced with entry, windows, and landscaping
- B2-6 access: confirm vehicle access does not require reversing onto or from an arterial road; confirm sight distance from exit driveway to street is adequate
- Bicycle: confirm secure (locked enclosure or cage), covered, and accessible location; 1 per dwelling minimum
- Accessible space: 2.4m wide + 2.4m shared zone, 5.4m long; overhead clearance 2.5m min; on accessible path to building entry

## Draft Response Logic
**Parking shortfall RFI:** Provide a parking assessment noting: (1) required spaces per Cl 52.06, (2) spaces provided, (3) shortfall, (4) Cl 52.06-5 justification — list nearest train, tram, bus stops and distance; confirm on-street availability in the street; note any TDM measures (bicycle storage, car share). Reference any council-specific local policy on parking waiver.

**Dominant garage RFI:** Demonstrate the "front of house" is not dominated by the garage — show window area, entry door location, landscaping, and facade articulation alongside the garage. If the lot is narrow, propose a recessed garage or single-width opening to reduce impact.

**Driveway grade query:** Provide a driveway grade cross-section showing all grade changes. Confirm each segment and transition against AS 2890.1 Table 3.1. Note vehicle clearance at grade break for low-clearance vehicles.
