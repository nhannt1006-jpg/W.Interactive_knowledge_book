# Building Regulations — Envelope (Walls, Roof, Windows, Waterproofing)

## AI Summary
The building envelope covers external walls, roof, windows, doors, and waterproofing. These are primarily WD (building permit) items governed by the NCC and Australian Standards, but several also appear in planning assessments — particularly cladding materials (neighbourhood character, BAL rating), window placement (overlooking, daylight), and garage doors (streetscape). Waterproofing is a critical defect risk.

## Common Council / Project Issues
- Cladding materials: TP assessors check neighbourhood character (does the proposed material match the established palette?); BAL ratings require non-combustible materials in affected zones per AS 3959
- Insulation (NCC H6): R-value requirements are often under-specified in early-stage documentation; thermal bridging through steel studs must be accounted for in the NatHERS model
- Box gutters: internal box gutters are a common failure point — inadequate falls, blocked overflows, and inadequate overflow relief height are recurring defects
- Window safety glazing: glazing in doors, adjacent to doors, and below 500mm from FFL must be safety glass per AS 1288; missed on drawings = defect at inspection
- Wet area waterproofing: AS 3740 is frequently not followed — membrane height (1800mm in shower recess), full junction tape, and certificate of compliance are required
- External deck waterproofing: abutment detail at wall is critical — membrane must turn up behind cladding; missing flashing = water entry after handover
- DPC: missing or discontinuous DPC is a common defect in masonry construction; step-down DPC at changes in floor level is often omitted

## What to Check
- External cladding: check if site is in BMO or BAL-rated bushfire zone; confirm proposed material is permitted under AS 3959 for the BAL rating; check HO/DDO/NCO for material preferences or restrictions
- Wall insulation: confirm R-value per NCC H6 Table H6D3 for Climate Zone 6 (Victoria); confirm NatHERS model matches drawings
- Roof drainage: size gutters and downpipes per AS 3500.3 (catchment area × rainfall intensity); confirm falls minimum 1:100; box gutters require overflow relief
- Box gutter overflow: confirm overflow scuppers or weir are set at a level that allows drainage before water enters the building; confirm overflow capacity equals or exceeds inflow
- Window safety glazing: mark all hazardous locations on drawings (within 300mm of floor, adjacent to doors, in doors, frameless balustrades); confirm AS 1288 glazing type
- Window overlooking: check window placement against B4-4 (overlooking) and B3-9 (daylight to new windows); resolved at planning stage but implemented in WD
- Wet area waterproofing: confirm membrane system per AS 3740; specify Class 1 shower recess (1800mm high); specify junction tape and bond breaker at all junctions
- External deck/balcony: confirm falls ≥ 1:100 to drain; confirm membrane turns up at wall junction ≥ 150mm behind cladding; specify flashing detail
- DPC: confirm continuous DPC at base of all masonry walls; weep holes at 450mm centres for cavity brick; step-down DPC at all floor level changes

## Draft Response Logic
**Cladding BAL query:** Confirm the BAL rating from the CFA report. Check AS 3959 Table 2 for the applicable construction requirements at that BAL level. Confirm the proposed cladding system meets the ember, radiant heat, and flame contact requirements for the BAL rating.

**NCC H6 insulation query:** Provide a wall section showing: (1) cladding, (2) cavity or sarking, (3) insulation batt (with R-value), (4) framing, (5) internal lining. Confirm total R-value accounts for thermal bridging through studs (use the NatHERS model's validated values).

**Box gutter RFI:** Provide the hydraulic design calculation (catchment area, rainfall intensity, gutter capacity) and confirm overflow relief height, dimensions, and that overflow discharges externally. Provide a section detail at overflow relief location.

**Waterproofing query:** Reference the AS 3740 certificate of compliance from the waterproofer. Confirm membrane type, height, junction treatment, and that the certificate is on file for the building surveyor.
