# Interiors, Services & Accessibility

## AI Summary
Covers NCC habitable room standards, accessible design (AS 1428.1), hydraulic and mechanical services, fire safety systems, and external works (fencing, pedestrian paths, street integration). These are primarily building permit (WD) items. Accessibility requirements apply to Class 2 buildings. Services coordination (hydraulic, mechanical, electrical) is critical at building permit stage to avoid conflicts with fire separation and structural elements.

## Common Council / Project Issues
- Accessible path of travel (Class 2): the accessible path from carpark and street to every dwelling is commonly undercoordinated — kerb ramps, threshold levels, and lift provisions need to be resolved before permit lodgement
- Adaptable dwellings: NCC requires a proportion of Class 2 dwellings to be adaptable (AS 4299 Class C minimum for visitability); reinforcing in bathroom walls and wider doorways must be shown on drawings
- Stair dimensions: rise 115–190mm, going 240–355mm for NCC Class 1; open risers not permitted in Class 2 common areas; handrail both sides if width >1m
- Accessible bathroom (AS 1428.1): 1500mm turning circle, hobless shower, grab rail backing plates, basin height — often under-specified
- Smoke alarm wiring: NCC requires 240V hardwired interconnected alarms in Class 1 buildings — 10-year battery backup alarms alone do not comply
- Exhaust ventilation: rangehood must duct to outside — recirculating types do not satisfy NCC H3.4; bathroom and toilet fans must also duct externally
- Noise from services (B5-6): AC condensers placed on roof or adjacent to bedroom windows generate objections; positioning and/or acoustic screening must be shown in planning application
- Pool/spa barriers: any pool or spa >300mm depth requires a compliant barrier per AS 1926.1; gates must be self-closing and self-latching; common defect at occupancy inspection
- Street integration (B3-3): new dwellings must address the street — active frontage (windows, entry), not a blank fence or garage wall

## What to Check
- Habitable rooms: confirm minimum areas (NCC H3.2) — bedroom min 7m², main bedroom 9m², living 10m²; ceiling height ≥ 2.4m in habitable rooms (for 2/3 of floor area)
- Room depth (B3-8): depth ÷ ceiling height ≤ 2.5 for single-aspect rooms (planning stage); confirm natural light compliance
- Stair: mark rise/going on drawings; confirm handrail height 865–1000mm; confirm headroom ≥ 2.0m; no open risers in Class 2 common areas
- Accessible path (Class 2): trace path from carpark/street entry to each dwelling; confirm maximum grade ≤ 1:20, no lips or steps, tactile indicators at hazards; lift if more than one storey
- Adaptable dwelling schedule: confirm required number; show reinforcing in shower walls (100mm framing, backing plates); confirm wider doorways (≥ 820mm clear)
- Storage (B3-11): ≤2 bed = 6m³, 3+ bed = 8m³; confirm location is accessible, not under stairs
- Smoke alarms: confirm 240V hardwired interconnected type; locate in each storey, corridor, and each bedroom or within 3m of bedroom door
- Exhaust fans: bathroom 25 L/s, kitchen 40 L/s, toilet 10 L/s; all ducted to outside
- AC condenser location: confirm screened from neighbour view and separated from bedroom windows; note B5-6 noise standard
- Bin storage: confirm number of bins, path to kerbside, and enclosure is not streetscape-dominant
- Street integration (B3-3): confirm windows and entry face the street; no blank walls to primary frontage

## Draft Response Logic
**Accessible path RFI:** Provide a path of travel plan tracing from the street boundary to each dwelling entry; annotate: surface type, grade at each section, level changes (ramped or flush), tactile indicator locations. Confirm compliance with AS 1428.1 at each point.

**Adaptable dwelling query:** Confirm which dwellings are designated adaptable; provide a schedule noting: Class (B or C), reinforcing wall locations, doorway clear widths, shower type (hobless), basin height, and WC dimensions. Confirm AS 4299 compliance.

**Noise from services (B5-6):** Provide the AC condenser location on the site plan. Confirm: distance from nearest habitable room window (own and neighbour's), screening proposed, and acoustic attenuation if required. If >45 dBL at boundary, provide an acoustic consultant's confirmation.

**Pool barrier query:** Confirm barrier height ≥ 1200mm, no climbable objects within 900mm of fence, gate is self-closing and self-latching at least 600mm from any corner. Provide AS 1926.1 checklist or private building surveyor certificate.
