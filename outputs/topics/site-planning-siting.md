# Site Planning — Siting Standards

## AI Summary
Covers the core ResCode Clause 55 neighbourhood character standards that govern how a building sits on its lot and relates to the street and neighbours. These apply to all two-or-more-dwelling applications (B2 standards) and are typically the first group of conditions tested in a planning permit assessment. Single-dwelling applications use equivalent A-standards under Clause 54.

## Common Council / Project Issues
- Street setback averaging produces a non-obvious result where adjoining lots have inconsistent setbacks — council may request a survey or notation showing the calculation
- Side/rear setback formula confusion: B2-3 offers two calculation paths (B2-3.1 formula or B2-3.2 flat rates) — councils expect consistency across all boundaries
- Building height — mandatory limits in NRZ (9m) and GRZ (11m) under VC228; AHD of natural ground level must be labelled on all elevations
- Site coverage — councils flag when garages, verandahs, or carports are excluded from the calculation; all roofed structures count
- Walls on boundaries — common point of RFI: councils ask for the length formula and will check existing wall length on the adjacent lot
- Front fence — DDO or HO schedules often override the B2-8 default; always check the overlay schedule first

## What to Check
- Street setback: measure from title boundary (not kerb) to nearest wall face — eaves excluded. Required setback = average of two closest walls on adjoining lots (up to 9m)
- Eave encroachment: ≤ 2.5m into setback AND eave height ≤ 3.6m above NGL — both conditions must be met
- Side setback (B2-3.1): 1m + (0.3 × metres above 3.6m height) up to 6.9m wall height; above 6.9m, add 1m per extra metre
- Side setback (B2-3.2 alternative): 3m (non-south) or 6m (south) for walls up to 11m; 4.5m / 9m above 11m
- Building height: confirm zone mandatory limit (NRZ 9m / GRZ 11m / RGZ 13.5m / HCTZ per schedule); show AHD on elevations; check if slope on site triggers the 10m allowance (≥ 2.5° across any 8m section)
- Site coverage: total all roofed areas including garage, carport, verandah, shed ÷ site area × 100
- Zone limits: NRZ 60%, GRZ 65%, RGZ/MUZ/HCTZ 70% — schedule can only tighten, never loosen
- Walls on boundary: max length = greater of (10m + 25% of remaining boundary length) or existing wall on adjacent lot; avg height ≤ 3.2m, max 3.6m
- Front fence: solid max 1.2m in front setback; above that must be visually permeable
- Tree canopy (B2-7): confirm yard dimensions and deep soil volumes are sufficient; check council local policy for minimum canopy numbers

## Draft Response Logic
**Street setback RFI:** Provide a table showing: Address of both adjoining lots, measured setback of each front wall, average calculation, and resulting required setback. Note any eave encroachment separately.

**Side setback query:** State which path is being applied (B2-3.1 or B2-3.2) and show the formula applied at each level change. If B2-3.2 is used, note it applies uniformly to all boundaries (not per-boundary choice).

**Building height dispute:** Confirm the NGL datum used (surveyor-certified AHD), show ridge/parapet height minus NGL at each measurement point. If slope triggers the 10m allowance, show the 8m cross-section profile.

**Site coverage challenge:** Provide an area schedule listing every roofed structure, dimensions, and area. Exclude only unroofed decks and uncovered paths explicitly.
