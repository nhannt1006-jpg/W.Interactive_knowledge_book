# Tree Canopy & Landscaping

## AI Summary
Tree canopy is addressed at two levels: Standard B2-7 under Clause 55 (new under VC276) requires space and soil volume for canopy tree planting as part of all two-or-more-dwelling applications. Separately, existing significant tree removal may trigger a planning permit under Cl 52.17, VPO, SLO, or ESO. Both issues need to be resolved before permit lodgement.

## Common Council / Project Issues
- B2-7 is a new standard (VC276) and many applicants omit it entirely — councils are issuing RFIs for canopy planting plans and soil volume calculations
- "Deep soil zone" requirements: sufficient soil volume (typically ≥ 8m² free of impervious surface and ≥ 0.5m deep, or per council DDO) to support a canopy tree
- Some councils have local policies in DDO or Cl 22 specifying minimum canopy numbers (e.g. 1 tree per dwelling, or 1 per frontage metre)
- Front setback canopy: if the entire front setback is paved/garaged, B2-7 compliance is difficult — this is increasingly flagged
- Easements: trees cannot be planted over sewer or drainage easements; soil volume in these areas does not count
- Rear setbacks used for deep soil: confirm the setback area is free of basement slabs, pits, or deep services that reduce effective soil depth
- Significant tree removal: if a protected tree is within the TPZ of any proposed works, an arborist report (AS 4970) is required; some councils require a tree management plan at permit stage

## What to Check
- B2-7 compliance: identify a planting zone with ≥ 8m² unobstructed at surface and ≥ 0.5m deep soil (or council minimum); confirm it is not over easements, basement, or hard paving
- Check if council DDO or local policy specifies minimum tree numbers or species type (indigenous preferred in many councils)
- If removing trees: check if site is within a VPO or SLO area; check Cl 52.17 native vegetation triggers; identify any council-listed significant trees (council's ecological management layer or Local Law)
- Confirm Tree Protection Zone (TPZ = 12 × trunk diameter) for any retained tree; no works within TPZ without arborist sign-off
- Check front setback: if less than 3m of clear deep soil is available in the front setback, alternative rear or side planting may need to be justified
- Letterboxes: all dwellings must have accessible letterboxes at the street — confirm separate from tree planting zones

## Draft Response Logic
**B2-7 RFI:** Provide a landscape plan (or annotated site plan) showing: (1) proposed canopy tree location(s), (2) deep soil zone boundary with dimensions, (3) soil depth confirmation (≥ 0.5m from finished surface level — confirm no basement or slab below), (4) species proposed (indigenous preferred). Note any council-specific requirements from local policy or DDO schedule.

**Tree removal — significant tree:** Provide an arborist report (AS 4970) confirming species, health, structural rating, and TPZ. If removal is necessary, justify why retention is not feasible (conflict with slab, insufficient setback) and offer a replacement planting strategy.

**TPZ conflict query:** Confirm works within TPZ will be managed per the arborist's tree management plan — hand excavation within TPZ, no storage of materials under canopy, protection fencing at TPZ boundary.
